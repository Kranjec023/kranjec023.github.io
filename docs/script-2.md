## Script

> "Most developers don't understand business. I do. — I started in IT at college,

> So I completed a full-stack bootcamp, built real applications with the MERN stack,

> I thrive in teams — finance desks, dev squads, you name it.

**⏱ Estimated time: ~47 seconds**

---

## Delivery Tips

- **"Most developers don't understand business. I do."** — slow, confident, look straight at the camera
- **"But tech never left me"** — pause before this, let the transition land emotionally
- **"MERN stack, Java, C#, Python"** — clear and deliberate, recruiters are listening for these
- **"I thrive in teams"** — smile here, lean slightly forward, use a hand gesture
- **"debug at 2am and love it"** — big smile, this is your humour moment
- **"debug in three languages"** — keep the smile going, it always lands
- **"I don't just write code"** — slow way down, this is your closing punch
- **"Let's connect"** — direct eye contact, confident smile, stop there — **no wink**

| Line | Why it works |
