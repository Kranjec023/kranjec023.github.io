# Elevator Pitch Script — Final Version
**Frédérick Kranjec-Larose — 45-50 Second Video**

---

## Script

> "Most developers don't understand business. I do — I started in IT at college,
> earned a bachelor's in accounting at UQAM, and spent 3 years in finance at RBC and Aviso Wealth.
> But tech never left me. So I completed a full-stack bootcamp,
> built real applications with the MERN stack, Java, C#, Python, and more —
> and I'm back where I was always meant to be.
> I thrive in teams — I've collaborated across finance desks, dev squads, and everything in between.
> Outside of code, I'm someone who loves pushing limits —
> whether that's traveling, hitting the gym, or solving a bouldering problem on the wall.
> I'm also a dog person and a math enthusiast —
> which probably explains why I'm drawn to clean logic, elegant solutions, and debugging at 2am.
> I speak French, English, and German — so I can debug in three languages.
> I don't just write code — I write code that makes business sense.
> Let's connect."

**⏱ Estimated time: ~57 seconds**

---

## Delivery Tips

- **"Most developers don't understand business"** — say this slowly and confidently, it's your hook
- **"But tech never left me"** — pause before this line, let the transition land
- **"MERN stack, Java, C#, Python"** — don't rush these, they're the keywords recruiters listen for
- **"debugging at 2am"** — smile here, it's your moment of humour
- **"debug in three languages"** — keep the smile going, it's a good laugh line
- **"I don't just write code"** — slow down, this is your closing punch
- **"Let's connect"** — look straight at the camera, smile, and stop there
